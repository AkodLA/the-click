## 6. The question (you probably get one) · answer in 15–20 seconds

**How to answer any question:** 1) short answer first, 2) one proof from the app, 3) stop. If it's deep tech, say «Akram built that part» and hand over. If you don't know: «Good question. We haven't tested that yet; it's on our list for the pilot.» Never invent a number.

### 🧑‍💼 Investor / business
- **«How do you make money?»** «Free to start, with a daily voice limit. Snakk Plus is 99 kroner a month for unlimited practice and Tolk. And workplaces pay per seat for their teams. Voice minutes are our main cost, so the free tier is limited.»
- **«Who are your first customers?»** «Two groups: people new in Norway who want to speak, and workplaces with multilingual teams: warehouses, nursing homes, schools. A workplace already has the problem *today*: staff who don't understand each other.»
- **«How are you different from other language apps?»** «Most apps teach textbook sentences you tap and read. Snakk makes you **speak**, in real situations, and tells you **which words fit which situation**. And no learning app also interprets for you until you're ready.»
- **«Why not just use Google Translate?»** «A translator helps you today, but you never learn. Snakk does both: Tolk helps you now, the tutor teaches you so you don't need Tolk tomorrow.»
- **«What's next?»** «A pilot with one workplace, a native speaker checking all content, and more languages: the engine works for any language, Norwegian is just first.»

### ⚖️ Lawyer / privacy
- **«What data do you store?»** «A random ID, a nickname you choose, and your progress. No email, no real name, no audio. Notes stay on your own device.»
- **«Isn't Tolk replacing interpreters?»** «No. It says so on screen: it's a communication aid for everyday moments. When a school, doctor or NAV needs an interpreter, they must book a qualified one by law (tolkeloven). Tolk is for everything in between, like a chat at pickup or finding a pharmacy.»
- **«What about children interpreting for parents?»** «That's exactly what we want to stop. Since 2022 public services may not use children as interpreters (tolkeloven), but kids still end up doing it in everyday life. Tolk means the parent has their own help, so the child can just be a child.»

### 🔐 Ethical hacker / security
- **«Can I fake my XP or level?»** «No. The browser only reports *what happened*; our server decides XP, levels and test scores, and the browser can't change it. API keys never leave the server.»
- **«What did you do about security?»** «Strict security headers, every input validated, rate limits on everything that costs money, and we tried to cheat our own XP with a script: the server blocked it.»

### 🗣️ Language teacher / product
- **«Is the Norwegian correct?»** «Every phrase is checked by a native speaker on the team, and the tutor shows when to use a word and when *not* to. That's the part textbooks skip.»
- **«What about dialects?»** «We teach the Norwegian people actually use, mostly the Oslo region to start, and we show which words are slang or regional. Dialect packs are a natural next step.»
- **«How do you keep people motivated?»** «Small daily goals, streaks, a weekly league, and the tree: it grows when you practise and gets thirsty when you don't. People come back for it.»
- **«Why start with Norwegian?»** «We're in Norway, we have native speakers to check the content, and the need here is real: almost a million immigrants. Then the same engine works for any language.»

### 🤷 Wildcard
- **«What was the hardest part?»** «Making the voice tutor reliable in a loud room, and making it notice *which situation* you're in, not just whether a word is correct.»
- **«Did you really build all this in a weekend?»** «Yes, the four of us, this weekend. The voice is ElevenLabs; the app, the content, the security and the design are ours.»

## 7. Words to avoid
- ❌ «certified interpreter», «replaces interpreters» → ✅ «a communication aid for everyday moments».
- ❌ any number without a source (see `docs/PITCH.md`).
- ❌ «gives your exact CEFR level» → ✅ «**estimates** your level».
- ❌ «it understands every accent» → ✅ «it shows which words came through».
- ❌ naming or attacking a competitor → ✅ «most apps teach textbook sentences; we teach when to say what».
